What is AES-256 Encryption in PDFs?

Definition of AES-256 Encryption

Advanced Encryption Standard with a 256-bit key is the military-grade cryptographic algorithm used to secure PDF documents. It mathematically scrambles the file binary data, making it virtually impossible to crack without the password.
