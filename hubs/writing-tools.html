Writing Tools

Count words, measure readability, compare drafts, and clean up text — all in your browser, without an account.

These writing utilities are built around the tasks writers, students, editors, and content teams repeat daily: checking word and character counts against a limit, understanding how readable a draft is, spotting differences between two versions, and normalizing messy text. Everything runs client-side, so your drafts stay on your device.

Word Counter

Words, characters, sentences, reading time, and readability scores in real time.

Character Counter

Track character limits, dense sections, and live writing metrics as you type.

Case Converter

Flip text between lower, upper, title, and sentence case.

Text Diff Checker

Highlight the exact differences between two versions of a document.

Reverse Text

Reverse characters, words, or lines for puzzles and quick checks.

Text Replacer

Find and replace with plain-text or regular-expression matching.

Remove Duplicate Lines

Deduplicate lines with an optional case-insensitive mode.

Split Text

Split text by a delimiter into clean, numbered lines.

Join Text Lines

Join lines with a custom separator such as a comma or pipe.

Lorem Ipsum Generator

Create placeholder copy for mockups and layout testing.

Censor Text

Mask sensitive words or phrases in pasted text.

Typing Speed Test

Measure WPM, accuracy, consistency, and weak keys in one screen.
