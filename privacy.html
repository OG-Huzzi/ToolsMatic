Privacy Policy

ToolsMatic is designed to be privacy-first. This policy explains what data we handle and how we keep it minimal.

What we collect

  • Many tools process your text and selected files locally in your browser. ToolsMatic does not receive those inputs unless a tool clearly says otherwise.
  • Google Analytics receives basic usage information such as page views and device/browser data so we can understand site performance.
  • No account or login is required. File tools may ask you to select a local file, but local processing is not a server upload.

Cookies and storage

Some tools use localStorage in your browser to remember preferences (for example, saved favorites). We do not use these values for tracking.

Third-party services

We use Google Analytics for aggregated usage measurement. No advertising is currently displayed. Analytics operates under its own privacy policy and should not receive the text or files processed by local tools.

Data you provide

Do not paste sensitive personal, financial, or secret information into the tools if you are unsure about your device or network security. You are responsible for the content you process.

Security

We aim to maintain a secure site, but no system is perfectly secure. Use the tools at your own discretion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
