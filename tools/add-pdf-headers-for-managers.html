Why Add Headers and Footers to PDFs?

Headers and footers are essential elements of professional document formatting. They provide contextual information that appears consistently on every page, helping readers identify the document, its classification, its author, and its place within a larger collection. In professional, legal, and corporate environments, headers and footers are not optional decorations — they are requirements.

A law firm preparing exhibit documents for court needs to stamp "Exhibit A" or "Exhibit B" at the top of every page. A corporation distributing internal strategy documents needs to stamp "CONFIDENTIAL — Internal Use Only" as a header. A publisher preparing a manuscript needs running headers with the book title and chapter name. All of these scenarios require batch text injection across every page of a PDF.

Common Header and Footer Use Cases

Confidentiality and Classification Labels

Government agencies, law firms, and corporations routinely classify documents as Confidential, Secret, Top Secret, Internal Only, or Draft. These classification labels must appear on every page of the document, not just the first page, to comply with information security policies. ToolsMatic stamps the label across every page in a single operation.

Legal Exhibit Marking

During legal proceedings, documents submitted as evidence must be clearly marked with exhibit numbers. Each page of each exhibit needs the same label. Manually adding exhibit markers to a 200-page document is impractical. ToolsMatic automates this process, stamping the exhibit label on every page instantly.

Company Branding

Businesses often want their company name, logo text, or website URL to appear at the top or bottom of every page in reports, proposals, and client deliverables. This reinforces brand identity and makes it easy to identify the source of a printed document.

Date and Version Tracking

Adding the date or version number as a footer helps recipients identify which version of a document they are viewing. This is especially important for documents that go through multiple revisions, such as contracts, specifications, and policy manuals.

How Text Injection Works

ToolsMatic uses pdf-lib to inject true vector text into every page of your PDF. Unlike watermarking tools that overlay text at an angle across the page center, headers and footers are precisely positioned at the top and bottom margins with horizontal alignment. The text is rendered using Helvetica, a standard PDF base font that does not need to be embedded, keeping file sizes minimal.

For each page, the tool calculates the exact coordinates based on the page dimensions, the specified font size, and the selected alignment (left, center, or right). The text is drawn directly into the PDF page stream, which means it is searchable, selectable, and accessible to screen readers. It is not a flattened image overlay.

Privacy and Security

Documents that need confidentiality labels are, by definition, confidential. Uploading them to a third-party server to add a header defeats the purpose of the classification. ToolsMatic processes everything locally in your browser. Your document is read into browser memory, modified using client-side JavaScript, and saved back to your device. The file never touches a network connection.

This local-first architecture makes ToolsMatic the only safe option for adding classification labels to documents that contain genuinely sensitive information. No server operator ever sees your file.
