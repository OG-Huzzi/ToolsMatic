Why Compare PDF Files?

PDF comparison is useful whenever you need to verify what changed between two versions of a document. Legal reviews, policy revisions, technical specs, and editorial workflows all depend on spotting differences quickly and accurately.

ToolsMatic compares both documents entirely inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, which makes it practical for confidential contracts, internal reports, and sensitive drafts.

How to Compare Two PDFs on ToolsMatic

  • Step 1 - Upload both PDFs: Add the original PDF on the left and the modified version on the right.
  • Step 2 - Choose a mode: Use Side by Side for visual review, Overlay Diff for page stacking, or Text Diff for extracted text comparison.
  • Step 3 - Navigate pages: Move page by page with synchronized controls that keep both documents aligned.
  • Step 4 - Copy the report: In Text Diff mode, copy a plain-text comparison report to your clipboard.

Three Ways to Compare PDFs

Side by Side Comparison

Both PDFs render at the same scale and scroll together. This is the fastest way to inspect layout shifts, changed pages, or formatting differences.

Overlay Diff

Overlay mode stacks one PDF on top of the other. Adjust opacity and blend mode to expose layout changes, shifted graphics, or visual edits.

Text Diff

Text Diff extracts lines from both PDFs and compares them using an LCS-based approach. Additions are green, deletions are red, and changed words are highlighted in amber.
