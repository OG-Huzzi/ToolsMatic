Why Use ToolsMatic Reader?

ToolsMatic Reader lets you view PDF files entirely in your browser using the open-source PDF.js library. No files are ever uploaded to our servers — your documents stay on your device, making it ideal for confidential contracts, private records, or sensitive business content.

With features like zoom controls, text search, page thumbnails, and print support, ToolsMatic Reader provides a full-featured viewing experience without compromising your privacy.

How to Use the PDF Reader

  • Step 1 — Upload: Drag and drop your PDF or click to browse. Files are validated locally and rendered using PDF.js in your browser.
  • Step 2 — Navigate: Use Prev/Next buttons, arrow keys, Page Up/Down, or swipe gestures to move between pages. Click thumbnails in the sidebar for quick jumps.
  • Step 3 — Zoom and search: Adjust zoom with +/- buttons or the percentage selector. Use the search bar to find text, with highlighted matches and next/previous navigation.
  • Step 4 — Print or download: Click Print or press Ctrl+P to open your browser's print dialog. Download the original file anytime with the Download button.

Viewer Features Explained

Page Navigation

Navigate efficiently using multiple methods: toolbar buttons, keyboard shortcuts (Arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End), or the thumbnail sidebar. The page indicator shows your position and allows direct page input.

Zoom Controls

Adjust zoom from 50% to 200%, or use Fit Width to match your viewport width, or Fit Page to show the entire page. Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd + +/- to zoom, Ctrl/Cmd + 0 for actual size.

Text Search

Find text anywhere in the document with the search bar. Matches are highlighted in yellow. Toggle case-sensitive search for precise matching. Navigate results with Next/Prev buttons or keyboard arrows.

View Options

Toggle Continuous scroll for seamless reading or single-page view for focused navigation. Enable Night Mode to invert colors for comfortable reading in low-light conditions.

Sidebar Panels

Thumbnails: Clickable page previews for visual navigation. Outline: PDF bookmarks and hierarchical structure (if available). Attachments: List of embedded files (if any).
