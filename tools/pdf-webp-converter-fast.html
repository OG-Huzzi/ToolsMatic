Why Convert Between PDF and WebP?

Converting between PDF and WebP formats serves different needs. Extract PDF pages as WebP images to use in web projects, social media, or presentations where image formats are required. Combine WebP images into a PDF to create shareable documents, reports, or portfolios. WebP offers 25-35% smaller file sizes than JPG at equivalent quality, making it ideal for web delivery.

ToolsMatic performs all conversions entirely inside your browser using PDF.js, Canvas API, and jsPDF. No file is ever uploaded to any server, making it ideal for confidential documents, private photos, or sensitive business content.

PDF to WebP: Format & Quality Options

WEBP (Recommended)

Best for web use. WEBP typically produces files 25-35% smaller than JPG at equivalent visual quality, with support for both lossy and lossless compression. Ideal when you need images for websites, apps, or digital sharing where file size matters.

JPG (JPEG)

Best for pages with photographs, complex graphics or colorful content. JPG is universally supported but introduces compression artifacts at lower quality settings. Choose JPG when maximum compatibility is required.

PNG

Best for pages with text, diagrams, charts or sharp geometric content. PNG is lossless — no quality degradation at any setting — but produces larger files. Ideal when you need pixel-perfect reproduction or transparency support.

Resolution Guide (PDF→WebP)

Resolution determines how many pixels the output image contains. Higher resolution means sharper images that look good when zoomed in or printed, but larger file sizes.

  • 1x (96 DPI): Screen quality. Good for web display or quick sharing. Smallest file size.
  • 2x (192 DPI): Standard print quality. Good for home printing and most professional uses. Recommended for most users.
  • 3x (288 DPI): High quality print. For large format printing or when you need maximum detail. Largest file size.

WebP to PDF: Page Layout Options

Page Size

Choose A4 (210×297mm) for international documents, Letter (8.5×11in) for US standard, or enter custom dimensions in millimeters or inches for precise control.

Orientation

Toggle between Portrait (taller than wide) and Landscape (wider than tall) to match your image aspect ratio or document requirements.

Margins

Select None (0mm), Small (10mm), Medium (20mm), or Large (30mm) margins to control whitespace around images. Larger margins improve readability for printed documents.

Image Fit

Choose "Fit to Page" to scale images proportionally within margins, "Original Size" to place images at native resolution, or "Stretch to Fill" to fill the entire page (may distort aspect ratio).
