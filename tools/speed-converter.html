Batch conversion is one of the biggest practical advantages

One of the most overlooked features in conversion tools is batch mode. A large number of real tasks involve lists, not single values. You may have a shipping sheet with multiple weights, a materials list with multiple lengths, a QA checklist with several temperatures, or a migration worksheet with many storage values. Re-entering the same from and to units for every row is slow and error-prone. Batch conversion solves that by letting you keep the same domain and unit pair while converting one value per line. That kind of feature is not flashy, but it is exactly the kind of capability that makes a converter genuinely better for work.

Combined with favorites and history, batch mode becomes even more useful.
