Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are these conversions?

All linear conversions use standard factors; temperature uses exact equations for Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin mappings.

Can I convert engineering units like pressure and energy?

Yes. Pressure includes Pa, bar, psi, atm; energy includes J, kJ, cal, kcal, Wh, and kWh.

Does this support metric and imperial systems?

Yes. The tool includes both metric and imperial units across all major categories.

What is formula view?

Formula view shows the conversion route and factors used, so you can verify results and document assumptions.

Can I process many values at once?

Yes, use Batch Converter with one input per line to convert datasets quickly.

Is this tool private?

Conversions are performed in-browser and saved data remains local to your device storage.
